Frequently Answered Questions

Everything you need to know regarding Extrait de Parfum concentration, pulse point rituals, bottle longevity, and delivery.

What makes Honey Trap an "Extrait De Parfum" rather than standard EDP? expand_more

Standard Eau de Parfum (EDP) formulations typically contain between 15% to 18% fragrance essence. Honey Trap is compounded at an artisanal 35% pure fragrance oil concentration. This elevated density slows evaporation, providing a velvet-like adherence on skin with minimal alcohol sharpness and rich projection that develops smoothly over 12 to 14 hours.

How long does Honey Trap linger on skin, hair, and clothing? expand_more

On moisturized pulse points, Honey Trap projects intensely for the first 4 to 6 hours, followed by a warm, intimate sillage lingering well past 12 hours. On natural fabrics (cotton shirts, cashmere scarves, blazer lapels), the base notes of golden amber resin, cocoa truffle, and bourbon vanilla frequently remain detectable for over 24 to 48 hours.

How should I apply Honey Trap for optimal projection without wasting juice? expand_more

Hold the flacon 6 inches away and target warm vascular pulse points: the sides of your neck, behind the earlobes, collarbones, and inner wrists. Important: Never rub your wrists together, as friction crushes delicate top notes like bitter orange blossom and candied ginger. Allow the mist to settle naturally.

How does the 100% Scent Love Guarantee & Discovery Vial work? expand_more

Every order of our 50ml or 100ml flacons includes a complimentary 2ml Discovery Vial. When your package arrives, open and test the miniature vial first. If the scent does not completely enchant you, you may return the unopened, sealed full flacon within 14 days for a full refund or exchange with zero hassle.

How should I store my luxury perfume flacon? expand_more

Keep your flacon in a cool, dark wardrobe or drawer away from direct sunlight, humid bathrooms, and extreme temperature fluctuations. Ultraviolet rays and heat degrade delicate organic floral molecules. Properly stored, Honey Trap will deepen in character and retain its full potency for 3+ years.

What are the dispatch and courier delivery times? expand_more

Orders are packed in temperature-controlled protective transit boxing. In Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad/Rawalpindi, delivery takes 24 to 48 hours. Nationwide courier across other cities takes 2 to 4 business days. Orders above Rs. 1,500 receive 100% complimentary courier.
