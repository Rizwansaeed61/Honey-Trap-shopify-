## 🚀 How to Set Up in Your Shopify Store

### Step 1: Import the Products (Takes 30 seconds)
1. Go to your **Shopify Admin** (`https://admin.shopify.com`).
2. In the left navigation, click on **Products**.
3. Click the **Import** button in the top right.
4. Click **Add file** and select:
   - `scents-n-stories-products.csv` from your **Downloads** folder.
5. Check **"Upload and continue"** -> **"Import products"**.
   - All 13 products with multiple sizes, prices, barcodes, and photos will be populated instantly.

### Step 2: Upload the Shopify Theme (Takes 20 seconds)
1. In Shopify Admin, click **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Under Theme library, click **Add theme > Upload zip file**.
3. Select `scents-n-stories-shopify-theme.zip` from your **Downloads** folder.
4. Once uploaded, click **Actions > Publish** (or **Customize** to preview and edit with the live visual theme editor).
