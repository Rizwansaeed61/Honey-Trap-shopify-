Grasse Heritage & Artisanal Maceration

Every bottle of Honey Trap is formulated with pure perfume oils sourced directly from historic distillation houses in Grasse, France. The formulation undergoes a 6-week dark cold maceration process, maturing the amber resin and natural vanilla crystals to achieve optimal richness, sillage radiance, and lasting skin adherence without harsh chemical stabilizers.
